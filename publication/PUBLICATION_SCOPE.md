# Public snapshot scope

## Included

The primary `crates` implementation, UI, tools, complete `docs` directory, root research reports, two independent reviews with examined snapshots/probes, and the separate AI Code Factory research materials. Working changes present at copying time are included. Product defects were not fixed merely to manufacture a green publication result.

## Omitted

The original `.git`, private `.local`, worktrees, raw conversations and derivative session data, private operational state, the large `swarm_1k` runtime corpus, forensic/context archives, caches, installed dependencies, compiler outputs, and working databases. Detailed private filesystem inventories and worktree listings are also omitted. The owner retains these originals. This is the complete primary research source/document snapshot, not a byte-for-byte publication of a workstation.

Raw chat copies were also removed from nested historical snapshots. Historical reports may still refer to omitted private artifacts. Such unavailable evidence is distinct from a missing product implementation.

## Redaction

User/project paths, email addresses, machine-specific addresses and environment identifiers were anonymized. Credentials and hardware-identifying details are not intentionally included. Historical hashes and results describe the original material and do not authenticate files changed by redaction or translation. The public manifest records the actual publication contents.

The external-model helper and audit copies use `OMNIROUTE_API_KEY` instead of querying a private credential database. Cargo uses a local `target` directory.

## Release decisions

The package retains the R3 Apache-2.0 licensing release for original material and public attribution to Ignat, [@ignatXfire](https://x.com/ignatXfire). It includes the approved logo, poster, 88-second animated overview, and original 39:30 AI-generated audio podcast under `assets`, and links the owner's companion NotebookLM notebook with an explicit R2-corpus label. Implementation and test files are unchanged from R3. Third-party source terms and provenance limitations are recorded in [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md); the short video has separate [media credits](../assets/MEDIA_CREDITS.md), including CC BY 4.0 music. These third-party components are not relicensed as Apache-2.0. GitHub private vulnerability reporting must be enabled after the actual repository exists. The owner does not promise ongoing development. This local preparation does not create or publish a GitHub repository and does not verify or change the online notebook's access settings. The R3 validation reports remain historical; current packaging checks are recorded separately in [the README/media validation](README_MEDIA_VALIDATION.json).
