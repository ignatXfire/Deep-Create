The comments could not be dismissed by reference to previous successful tests: several new negative scripts did find errors. It is also impossible to take the entire review literally: the requirement to check the old publication only for the current unit would break the fixed historical results, and the shares of the weekly quota chosen by the checker are not the owner’s decision.

We preserve the original system: several lengthy works, precise connections between results, branches of research, independent planning within given limits, separate verification, controlled actions and development. We are correcting inconsistencies in the rules and strengthening the verification of their combinations. The complete system has not yet been implemented.

## Why correcting a publication is not limited to checking the current manufacturer

Let A release the result of revision 1, B commit it as input, then A moves to revision 2. The result of revision 1 does not become false just because of this change. Checking solely against A's current `accepted_result` would cause B to lose a valid pinned input.

Therefore, the publication has a clear preserved basis: the purpose and edition of the unit, the accepted package, the imprint, the check and the reference to the composition of the output. Their connections are checked by machine. A separate mutable record reports whether the old result has been revoked. It cannot be replaced by an old copy in a saved base.

In the current pure layer, this is a consistency check of the presented records. Retrieving actual stored records, byte immutability, read permissions, and a completed checkpoint still require C-03/C-04/C-08. Populating a public struct from your own Rust code is still possible: the absence of `Deserialize` prevents one dangerous shortcut, rather than turning the struct into a trusted source.

## Conditions and the unknown

A regular three-digit logical "and" does allow `False AND Unknown = False`. Our previous contract additionally requires not to bypass an unknown canonical basis and to preserve read dependencies even under a negative condition. This is different from calculating two independent mathematical values.

In the current design, the unknown is not promoted to truth, the conditional edge does not disappear from the test of the possible cycle, and changing the significant base invalidates the old branch choice. Semantics cannot be changed just for the sake of passing more papers. If the usual logical "and" is later required, it should be a separate versioned form with a proven list of dependencies and a reactivation check, and not a silent change from the previous `All`.

## Unsolvable external outcome: how to move on without inventing a fact

We distinguish between two independent questions: **what actually happened outside** and **how the owner wants to continue working**. The decision to stop waiting does not determine the external outcome.

Valid future owner commands:

1. Leave the branch awaiting reconciliation.
2. Close a branch with an explicitly written unknown. This is not the successful fulfillment of her obligation; the main work either retains the gap or receives a new version of the goal accepted by the owner.
3. Provide evidence for verification. The owner's text does not automatically turn into a supplier receipt; the source, type and boundaries of sufficiency are recorded.
4. Adopt an independent strategy. The new branch has its basis and limits, does not imply the success/absence of the previous action and does not mask a repetition of the same potential effect.

The old intention, late observations and uncertain expenditure remain. You cannot use the “close branch” button to set the flow rate to zero or release the reserve. If it is impossible to prove the upper bound, the unknown remains in the general accounting. Independent research work in other valid branches should not be stopped because of one such branch.

These commands are not yet connected to the application: they are an accepted refinement of the future life path C-05/C-09/C-13. The check will require a combination of branch closure, late confirmation, target change, and overall limit.

## Long-term supply: what is accepted from the offer and what is not

A fair queue selects the next valid move between existing jobs. She cannot know in advance that an important new task will appear in an hour. The reserve for the future is a separate preference of the owner, and not a hidden adjustment of the model to the importance of the work.

For future implementation: the rule is bound to the exact resource/connection and period, has an accepted owner event and edition. It may limit the access of ordinary works to part of the total balance. Admission to the protected part is given by a separate accepted rule of the owner; the model’s words “emergency task” do not give it. The overall hard cap and unknown historical costs apply on top of any allocation. The same single log is used; sub-inventory does not create cash or separate inconsistent write-offs.

Percentages 70/20/10 from review - illustration, not set. The subscription balance percentage cannot be considered an accurate known quota without a reliable accounting form and period. It is impossible to silently switch to a weak model due to the exhaustion of the reserve, contrary to the quality threshold. Before a separate contract and testing, neither hidden interest nor a non-working allocator were added to the code.

## Register of contracts and protection against forgotten restrictions

`contracts/contract-map.json` maps 12 important contracts to source types, test files, implemented parts, and required spaces. `tools/check-contract-map.mjs` checks the form, uniqueness, existence of links and the prohibition of unreasonable declaration of canonical readiness. Eleven negative/positive tests are checked by this inspector himself.

The controller does not understand all of Rust and does not prove the truth of the descriptions. The presence of a function name in a file does not replace its test. Running tests is done separately in a common pass. Currently, all registered complete contracts are marked as partial; this does not mean that there are no functions already performed.

Identifiers are validated against [RFC 9562 §5.7](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9562.html#section-5.7). It is the form that is being tested, not the watch's accuracy, uniqueness or authorship. The order of decisions is determined by the event log, not by UUID sorting. Text identifiers are not converted to JavaScript numbers.
