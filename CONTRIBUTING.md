# Continue Deep Create

Independent development is welcome under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE). You may fork, modify and redistribute the original project material, including commercially, subject to the license and existing third-party rights. No permission from Ignat is needed for a use already permitted by those terms.

## Maintenance model

Ignat is sharing the research and does not plan to be the ongoing application developer. This is an unfinished research snapshot, not a supported product. Issues and pull requests may remain unanswered. There is no commitment to review, merge, supply fixes, meet deadlines, or provide security support.

If you want dependable development, maintain an independent fork with clear ownership and its own policies. Reference the original snapshot and author, mark your changes, and do not imply that Ignat endorses your fork. Repository transfer or write access requires a separate explicit decision; it is not a prerequisite for a lawful fork.

## Useful contributions

A useful proposal identifies the contract, exact source revision, synthetic reproduction, expected behavior, observed result and evidence boundary. Retain actual commands, exit codes, artifact hashes and negative cases. Separate product defects from build-environment failures, provider outages and unknown external outcomes. An author's own "passed" statement is not independent acceptance.

Preserve unrelated changes and failure evidence. Inspect historical organization scripts before running them. Use isolated workspaces, synthetic data and your own authorized credentials. Never attach private sessions, secrets, personal databases or unrelated project content. Report sensitive vulnerabilities according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not in a public issue.

Submit only material you are entitled to contribute and identify its third-party sources and terms. Unless explicitly stated otherwise and agreed separately, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion are provided under the project's Apache-2.0 terms; no copyright assignment is requested. Existing third-party rights remain in force.
